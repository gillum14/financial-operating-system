# Purpose

The Retirement Experience helps users understand whether they are building sufficient long-term financial security.

Rather than displaying account balances alone, Athena translates retirement data into meaningful projections, readiness indicators, and actionable recommendations.

The Retirement Experience exists to replace uncertainty with confidence.

---

# Mission

Help every user answer one question:

> **"Am I on track to retire comfortably?"**

Every feature within the Retirement Experience should contribute toward answering this question.

---

# Product Philosophy

The Retirement Experience is built upon five principles.

## Simplicity Over Complexity

Retirement planning should be understandable without financial expertise.

---

## Preparation Over Prediction

Athena provides projections, not guarantees.

The goal is informed decision-making.

---

## Progress Over Perfection

Retirement readiness improves through consistent contributions.

Small improvements deserve recognition.

---

## Transparency Over Assumptions

Users should understand how every retirement projection is calculated.

---

## Long-Term Confidence

Retirement planning is measured in decades rather than months.

Athena should encourage sustainable habits rather than short-term optimization.

---

# Retirement Principles

The Retirement Experience should answer four questions.

- Am I on track?
- What happens if I continue?
- What happens if I improve?
- What's the best next step?

- Strong
- Building
- Needs Attention

- Savings rate
- Current assets
- Contribution consistency
- Estimated retirement expenses
- Time horizon

# Retirement Forecasting

Athena projects retirement outcomes based on current behavior.

Examples include:

- Estimated retirement age
- Estimated portfolio value
- Estimated monthly retirement income
- Withdrawal sustainability
- Confidence intervals

Forecasts should update automatically as financial behavior changes.

# Non-Goals

The Retirement Experience intentionally does not:

- Predict investment returns.
- Guarantee retirement outcomes.
- Replace licensed financial advice.
- Recommend specific securities.
- Encourage excessive investment risk.

The goal is long-term confidence through informed planning.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every Retirement Experience should answer one question:

> **"Do I feel more confident about my financial future than I did before opening Athena?"**

If the answer is no, the experience should be improved.

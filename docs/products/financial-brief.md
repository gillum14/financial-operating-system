# Purpose

The Financial Brief is Athena's primary communication system.

Rather than requiring users to interpret dashboards, charts, and financial reports, the Financial Brief delivers concise, personalized summaries that explain what changed, why it matters, and what actions will have the greatest positive impact.

Every Financial Brief should reduce complexity, increase understanding, and reinforce financial confidence.

The Financial Brief transforms financial data into meaningful conversations.

---

# Mission

Help every user answer three questions within seconds:

- What changed?
- What deserves my attention?
- What should I do next?

If the Financial Brief cannot answer these questions clearly, it has failed its purpose.

---

# Product Philosophy

The Financial Brief is built upon five principles.

## Clarity Over Complexity

Users should never need to interpret dozens of metrics.

Athena should identify what matters most and communicate it clearly.

---

## Insight Over Information

The Financial Brief exists to explain financial meaning, not simply display financial data.

Users should leave every briefing with a better understanding of their financial situation.

---

## Encouragement Over Judgment

Financial communication should remain constructive regardless of financial circumstances.

The goal is progress, never guilt.

---

## Action Over Observation

Every Financial Brief should provide at least one meaningful recommendation whenever an opportunity exists.

Insight without action creates little value.

---

## Consistency Builds Confidence

The Financial Brief should establish a predictable rhythm that encourages regular engagement without overwhelming users.

---

# Communication Principles

Every Financial Brief should follow the same communication hierarchy.

1. Current financial position
2. Meaningful changes
3. Important risks
4. Mission progress
5. Highest-impact recommendation

Information should always appear in order of importance rather than data source.

---

# Brief Types

## Daily Brief

Provides a concise overview of recent financial activity.

Typical content:

- Confidence Score changes
- Mission progress
- Cash flow updates
- New transactions requiring attention
- Recommended action

---

## Weekly Brief

Summarizes financial performance across the previous week.

Typical content:

- Spending trends
- Budget performance
- Investment activity
- Confidence movement
- Mission progress

---

## Monthly Brief

Provides a comprehensive financial review.

Includes:

- Income
- Expenses
- Savings
- Investments
- Net worth
- Budget adherence
- Confidence trend
- Mission completion
- Progress toward annual goals

---

## Event Brief

Generated when significant financial events occur.

Examples include:

- Mission completed
- Confidence level changed
- Large transaction detected
- Emergency fund milestone reached
- Investment milestone achieved
- Unusual spending identified

---

# Financial Brief Structure

Every Financial Brief should follow a consistent format.

## Greeting

A concise introduction acknowledging the user.

Example:

> Good morning, Caitlin.

---

## Current Position

Summarize overall financial status.

Example:

> Your financial position remains strong.
>
> Confidence Score: 87 (+2)

---

## Key Changes

Highlight only the most meaningful developments.

Examples:

- Cash flow improved.
- Emergency fund reached 74%.
- Credit utilization decreased.
- Investment portfolio increased.

---

## Mission Progress

Summarize active mission updates.

Example:

Emergency Fund

74%

Estimated Completion

October 12

Projected Confidence Gain

+5

---

## Recommendation

Present the single highest-impact opportunity.

Example:

Transfer $250 to your emergency fund.

Estimated Confidence Gain

+2

---

## Closing

Finish with an encouraging summary.

Example:

You're making consistent progress toward long-term financial confidence.

---

# Confidence Updates

Whenever the Confidence Score changes, Athena should explain why.

Example

Confidence Score

84 → 86

Primary contributors:

- Positive monthly cash flow
- Lower credit utilization
- Increased emergency savings

Users should always understand the reason behind score movement.

---

# Mission Updates

Mission updates should communicate progress rather than percentages alone.

Example

Emergency Fund

73%

$10,950 of $15,000

Projected Completion

October 14

Confidence Gain

+5

---

# Recommendations

Recommendations should always prioritize impact.

Athena should recommend only the highest-value opportunity available.

Examples:

- Increase emergency savings
- Pay down high-interest debt
- Increase retirement contributions
- Reduce recurring spending
- Review uncategorized transactions

Each recommendation should explain why it matters.

---

# Alerts

Alerts should be rare and meaningful.

Examples:

- Negative cash flow
- Missed debt payment
- Unusual spending activity
- Rapid confidence decline
- Low emergency reserves

Alerts should never become notification spam.

---

# Personalization

Every Financial Brief should adapt to the user.

Factors include:

- Financial goals
- Mission priorities
- Confidence level
- Household structure
- Spending behavior
- Investment strategy
- Retirement stage

No two users should receive identical Financial Briefs.

---

# Dashboard Experience

The Financial Brief should be the first experience users encounter upon opening Athena.

Selecting any section expands into supporting detail.

Examples:

- Confidence details
- Mission Brief
- Spending analysis
- Forecast
- Transactions
- Investments

The Dashboard provides navigation.

The Financial Brief provides understanding.

# Non-Goals

The Financial Brief intentionally does not attempt to:

- Replace detailed financial reports.
- Display every transaction.
- Encourage constant financial monitoring.
- Overwhelm users with excessive metrics.
- Generate unnecessary notifications.

Its purpose is clarity, not volume.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every Financial Brief should answer one question:

> **"What is the most important thing I need to know right now?"**

If the answer cannot be communicated clearly in less than a minute, the Financial Brief should be simplified.

Athena exists to create confidence through understanding.

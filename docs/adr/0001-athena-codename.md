# Purpose

This Architecture Decision Record documents the adoption of **Athena** as the internal engineering codename for the Financial Operating System project.

---

# Status

**Accepted**

---

# Context

The project required a consistent internal identifier that could be used throughout engineering documentation, architecture discussions, issue tracking, and technical communication.

The public-facing name, **Financial Operating System**, accurately describes the application but is unnecessarily verbose for internal engineering use.

An internal codename provides a concise, stable identifier while allowing the public product name to evolve independently if needed.

---

# Decision

The internal engineering codename for this project shall be **Athena**.

The public product name shall remain **Financial Operating System**.

Engineering documentation may reference "Athena" after the initial project identification.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Atlas

Selected as an early finalist because it represented structure, organization, and long-term stability.

Rejected because the symbolism emphasized strength and support rather than thoughtful system design.

## Aegis

Considered due to its strong association with protection and cybersecurity.

Rejected because it focused too narrowly on security rather than the broader engineering goals of the project.

## Polaris

Considered because it symbolizes guidance and navigation.

Rejected because Athena more closely represents wisdom, strategy, craftsmanship, and engineering.

---

# Consequences

Positive:

- Establishes a concise engineering identity.
- Simplifies technical documentation.
- Separates internal engineering terminology from public branding.
- Provides a stable identifier for future architectural documentation.

Negative:

- New contributors must understand the distinction between the public product name and the internal codename.

No technical impact on implementation.
